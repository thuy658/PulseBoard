About PulseBoard

PulseBoard helps you follow soccer and basketball through a compact daily view, league tables, rankings, team details, match details, widgets, and notifications.

Common Questions

How do I change my favorite teams or leagues?

Open Profile, choose My Teams or My Leagues, and use Add Favorite to add or remove content. Changes are stored on your device.

Why do notifications or live activities not appear?

Check PulseBoard notification settings and iOS Settings for PulseBoard. Some features depend on your iOS version, device capabilities, and whether notifications are allowed.

How do I remove cached data?

Open Profile and select Clear Cache. Favorites and preferences remain available unless you remove PulseBoard from iOS.

How do I stop widget content or live activities?

Remove the PulseBoard widget from the iOS home screen or lock screen, or dismiss the live activity from the device. You can also uninstall PulseBoard to remove its local content.
