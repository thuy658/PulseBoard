PulseBoard

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Overview

PulseBoard is an iOS sports information tool that helps you view scores, schedules, standings, rankings, and related match information. This policy explains what information the app stores and processes on your device.

PulseBoard does not require you to create an account. It does not sell personal information, and this version does not use advertising tracking or behavioral advertising.

Local Data

Most information you configure in PulseBoard stays on your device:

  • Favorite teams and favorite leagues.
  • Preferred leagues, language, and notification preferences.
  • Cached sports data used to show recent content offline.

This data is stored in the app sandbox. If you enable widgets or live activities, a limited snapshot needed to render those system features is stored in the shared app group used by PulseBoard.

Notifications, Widgets, and Live Activities

  • Notification settings are stored locally. Notifications are scheduled on your device and may use iOS notification permissions.
  • Widgets may display a local snapshot of recent sports content and are updated using the shared app group.
  • Live activities may appear on the lock screen or Dynamic Island when iOS supports them and you choose to leave one active.

Data Retention and Removal

You can remove cached sports data by using Clear Cache in the app. Removing PulseBoard from iOS deletes the local app data. If you no longer want widget content or live activities to remain visible, you can remove the relevant widget or end the live activity from the device.

Children

PulseBoard is intended for general audiences. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and it does not require an account.

Changes to This Policy

This page may be updated when app features or data practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with a new update date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or PulseBoard, contact: support@pulseboard.app
